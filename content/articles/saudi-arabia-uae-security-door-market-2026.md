Saudi Arabia and the UAE remain the highest-value destinations for premium security doors — but they are also the most demanding. Success in 2026 belongs to suppliers who arrive with the right ratings, the right material and the right paperwork, not just the right price.

## Demand: Where the Volume Is

- Saudi giga-projects and housing programmes generating sustained residential and hospitality door demand
- The UAE's villa and branded-residence culture, where the entrance is a status purchase
- Hotel construction across both markets — dual fire + security specifications by default
- Renovation demand in mature Dubai and Abu Dhabi communities upgrading to smart entrances

## Specification Realities

Gulf projects specify hard requirements: civil-defence-recognised fire performance (90-minute doors are the practical benchmark on many tenders), verified burglar resistance, and increasingly a smart-access layer integrated with the home or property system. Suppliers without test certificates for all three simply do not reach the shortlist.

## Climate Is a Filter

Coastal Gulf air combines salt, heat and humidity — the exact conditions that destroy coated steel. This is why cast-aluminium construction has become the premium default: it cannot rust, holds rich finishes through years of exposure, and suits the sculptural design language Gulf villas favour. WONLY's export range is built for this profile — corrosion-proof cast aluminium, hurricane-rated wind Class 12, EN 1634 90-minute fire compliance for Gulf codes and GB 17565 Class A security.

## How Distributors Win Here

- Lead with certificates and the compliance pack — tenders are document-driven
- Stock premium finishes and offer bespoke sizes for villa and hospitality work
- Bundle smart locks and whole-home integration for developer differentiation
- Partner with a manufacturer offering project pricing, OEM options and 24-hour engineering response — WONLY supports Gulf partners with exactly this model

## FAQ

Do KSA and UAE require local approvals on imported doors? Fire-rated openings typically need civil-defence-recognised evidence; confirm each emirate's or municipality's acceptance route before shipping — WONLY supplies the documentation pack with project orders.
