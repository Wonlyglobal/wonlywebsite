Modern smart locks combine several access technologies so users can choose the method that suits the moment. Understanding how each one works helps you explain the value to your customers.

## Fingerprint Recognition

A capacitive or optical sensor reads the ridges of a finger and matches them against stored templates in under a second. Quality sensors handle dry, wet or worn fingerprints and store data only on the lock, not in the cloud.

## Face and Palm Recognition

3D face and palm-vein recognition let users unlock hands-free, which is ideal when carrying bags or groceries. Depth-sensing cameras resist being fooled by a photo, keeping convenience and security together.

## App and Remote Control

A companion app unlocks the door, issues time-limited passwords for guests or cleaners, and logs who came and went. Communication is encrypted so codes cannot be intercepted.

## Secure Fallbacks

Good locks never leave a user stranded. Common fallbacks include:

- An anti-peep PIN keypad
- A concealed physical key
- An emergency external power port
- Auto-relock and tamper alarms
