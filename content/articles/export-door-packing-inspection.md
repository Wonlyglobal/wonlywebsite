A packing review should connect each door to its opening schedule and agreed transport conditions. A clean crate alone does not establish that the supplied doors are complete or protected for the journey.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Match door marks, crate identifiers and quantities to the approved packing list.
- Photograph corners, finish surfaces and protective materials before closing the crate.
- Check separately packed hardware against the assembly list; control keys through an agreed handover process.
- Confirm lifting points, storage conditions and handling instructions with the supplier and carrier.
- Agree how arrival damage will be recorded and reported before dispatch.

## Record and Approval

Keep dated photographs, the packing-list revision and inspection disposition together. Request supplier approval for materials contacting the finish. Packaging cannot guarantee damage-free transit; responsibility and remedies need to follow the agreed purchase and transport arrangements.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
