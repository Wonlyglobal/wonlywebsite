A similar-looking hinge, closer or lock is not automatically an equivalent substitution. Review the effects on the approved door configuration before price or availability drives a replacement decision.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Record original part, proposed part, reason for change and affected door marks.
- Compare dimensions, fixing pattern, interfaces, function and maintenance requirements with supplier confirmation.
- Ask whether fire, acoustic, security or other project evidence needs reassessment; do not make that decision from appearance.
- Assign separate review roles for design, installation, evidence and commercial impact.
- Update the relevant drawings, schedules and sample approval only after written acceptance.

## Record and Approval

Maintain a substitution register with version, evidence references, open questions, cost/time impact and final approver. A supplier's 'equivalent' statement should identify what was compared and what was not. Hold the change if compatibility or evidence is unresolved. Retain the original approved reference so site teams can identify unauthorised substitutions during inspection.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
