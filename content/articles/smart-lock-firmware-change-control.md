A firmware change is a product change, even when the enclosure and model label look unchanged. Keep a controlled link between hardware, firmware, app or service dependencies and the agreed acceptance results.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Record the proposed version, release purpose and affected hardware or delivery batches.
- Ask which functions, integrations, settings and supported environments may change.
- Agree a regression matrix covering the project's actual credential, offline, power-recovery and service scenarios.
- Define approval, update responsibility and a supported recovery or rollback process with the supplier.
- Confirm how shipped units and service replacements will be identified after the change.

## Record and Approval

Maintain a release record with old/new versions, supplier notes, test references, known limitations, approval and deployment scope. Never publish credentials, signing material or customer identifiers in that record. A version number alone is not proof of security or compatibility. Do not require an unsupported rollback on every device: first confirm what recovery method the supplier permits and who is authorised to use it.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
