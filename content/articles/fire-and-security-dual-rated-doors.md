In hotels, hospitals and apartment buildings, many openings must satisfy two inspectors at once: the fire officer, who cares about minutes of integrity, and the security auditor, who cares about forced-entry resistance. A door that passes one and fails the other is a retrofit waiting to happen — the answer is a genuinely dual-rated doorset.

## Two Tests, One Construction

Fire performance (EN 1634) and burglar resistance (EN 1627 / GB 17565) are separate test regimes with different protocols. A dual-rated door is one construction that has passed both — which is an engineering achievement, because fire design favours intumescent seals and sacrificial layers while security design favours mass, bolts and rigidity. Only the test certificates prove the combination works.

## Where Dual Rating Is Effectively Mandatory

- Hotel perimeter, back-of-house and service entrances on escape routes
- Hospital ward entries, pharmacies and records rooms inside fire compartments
- Apartment entrance doors — fire-rated by code, security-rated by residents' expectations
- Server rooms, plant rooms and any secure room whose walls are fire compartment lines

## Specification Pitfalls to Avoid

- Two certificates from two different constructions — both must cover the same tested doorset
- Security hardware swapped onto a fire door after certification — this voids the fire rating
- A rated leaf in an unrated frame — the doorset is the certified unit
- Missing the closer and intumescent seals from the order — they are part of the tested set

## FAQ

Does adding a smart lock void a fire rating? It can, unless the lock is part of the tested configuration — specify smart access from the manufacturer's certified options rather than retrofitting.

Who should confirm the required minutes? The project's fire strategy document — take EI class and minutes per opening from it, then layer the security grade on top.
