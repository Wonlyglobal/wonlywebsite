Southeast Asia is one of the fastest-moving regions for residential security. For distributors, three characteristics make it a strong market for premium doors and smart locks.

## Urbanisation and New-Build Housing

Rapid urban growth across Vietnam, the Philippines, Indonesia and neighbouring markets is producing waves of condominium and landed-housing projects, each of which needs entrance doors and locks at scale.

## A Hot, Humid Climate

High humidity and coastal air make corrosion resistance essential. Cast-aluminium doors and well-sealed smart locks keep their finish and function far longer here than untreated steel or budget hardware.

## Fast Smart-Home Adoption

The region is mobile-first and quick to adopt app-based living. Fingerprint and app locks are increasingly expected in new condos, which supports bundled sales across doors, locks and home devices.
