A distributor sample should answer the questions that could otherwise become a repeated problem across a shipment. Select a representative configuration and involve the local installation and service team before approving a display-only sample.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Identify intended city, use, exposure and target customer; record assumptions rather than claiming one configuration suits all Mexico projects.
- Approve a sample drawing, finish reference, hardware set and language requirements before manufacture.
- Have the local installer review openings, handing, wall interface and service access.
- Record sample observations, limitations, corrections and the conditions for approving the next revision.
- Separate product approval from import, local regulatory and project acceptance checks performed by competent parties.

## Record and Approval

Use an approval sheet with sample ID, revision, photographs, reviewer, corrective action and production-release decision. State whether approval covers appearance only or the agreed functional checks. Do not copy the display sample into a bulk order without confirming the final specification, quantities, spare-parts plan and responsibilities for customer support.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
