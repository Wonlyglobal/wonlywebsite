A smart lock that doesn't talk to the alarm, a camera that doesn't know the door opened, a gas sensor that alerts a phone nobody checks — standalone gadgets create notification noise, not security. The real product is the ecosystem: devices that share context and act together, with the entrance door as its first and most important node.

## Why the Door Is the Hub Moment

Almost every meaningful home event passes the entrance: arrivals, departures, deliveries, attempted intrusions. An intelligent door — identity-aware through 3D face and fingerprint unlock, monitored by a frame camera, always powered — is the natural trigger for everything else: disarm on recognised entry, arm on departure, lights and climate to home mode, alerts when the door reports tampering.

## What an Integrated System Does That Gadgets Cannot

- Auto arm/disarm from door identity — no keypad ritual, no forgotten alarms
- Scene automation — entry triggers lighting, climate and voice-message playback
- Layered alerts — door tamper, motion, smoke, gas and water sensors reporting through one app with one logic
- Guardianship — elderly-fall detection, child-arrival notifications and package monitoring at the threshold

## Specifying an Ecosystem, Not a Shopping List

- One platform and app across door, locks, sensors and panels
- The entrance door as a powered, connected device — WONLY's smart doors run dual power (mains + backup) with Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/Zigbee connectivity
- Local operation for critical functions so security survives an internet outage
- Room-by-room sensor coverage: entry, smoke, gas, water, presence and emergency buttons
