The supporting construction is part of the installation problem, not a detail to leave until delivery. The same-looking opening may contain very different materials and fixing constraints.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Identify whether the support is concrete, masonry, a framed partition or a mixed construction; record condition and accessible build-up.
- Obtain the selected frame's installation requirements and the project engineer's requirements for the actual substrate.
- Define responsibility for reinforcement, openings, backing, perimeter treatment and concealed services before drilling is permitted.
- Ask who specifies fixings and verifies their suitability; do not substitute a generic anchor spacing or capacity.
- Check whether the proposed support and fixing arrangement are covered by the relevant doorset evidence or require review.

## Record and Approval

Use an interface record with wall reference, frame detail, fixing proposal, supporting evidence, engineer's review and site hold points. A drywall finish does not by itself identify the backing structure. Where information is missing, request a verified detail rather than designing the connection from a catalogue image. Inspection must occur before inaccessible work is closed.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
