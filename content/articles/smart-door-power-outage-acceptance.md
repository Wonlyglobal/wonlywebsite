Power-outage acceptance should be planned for the exact door, lock, controller and backup arrangement. It is not a live experiment to improvise on an occupied escape route.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Have the responsible designer and supplier identify supply sources, affected functions and the approved safe test procedure.
- Distinguish loss of external supply, depleted backup and loss of communications; do not assume they produce the same result.
- Define expected entry, exit, local operation, indication and recovery behaviour for each agreed condition.
- Assign a competent person to conduct the test and a safety responsible person to protect access and escape throughout.
- Record restoration checks, remaining faults and the decision required before the door returns to normal service.

## Record and Approval

The acceptance record should identify door mark, configuration revision, condition, expected result, observed result, operator and approver. Do not disable required protections or interrupt an occupied escape arrangement to follow a generic checklist. Any conflict between security, accessibility and escape requirements must be resolved by the qualified project team; no particular emergency function is implied for an unconfirmed model.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
