A coastal maintenance plan should follow the actual coating, hardware and exposure, not a universal cleaning interval. Start with the finish supplier's instructions and a record that makes changes visible over time.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Identify door mark, coating system, finish reference, hardware materials and exposure location.
- Obtain approved cleaning products, methods, inspection frequency and restrictions from the relevant suppliers.
- Record deposits, damage, discolouration and seal condition with comparable dated photographs.
- Assign who decides whether damage requires repair, specialist review or a warranty enquiry; do not improvise chemical treatment.
- Keep maintenance actions, products used and follow-up observations alongside the original condition record.

## Record and Approval

Use a log containing date, location, observed condition, exposure changes, action, material used, operator and next review. Avoid publishing private property details. A salt-spray claim or a coastal sales description does not replace a care plan for the supplied finish. Ask about local service and compatible repair materials before ordering, and confirm warranty terms separately.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
