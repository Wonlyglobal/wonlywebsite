A working online demonstration does not show what happens when the network disappears. Define the expected offline behaviour for the proposed model and firmware before accepting a project sample.

## Practical Review Checklist

- List each credential type and ask whether it is intended to work without the network.
- Distinguish internet loss, local network loss and power loss; test them separately under an authorised plan.
- Agree what should happen to schedules, clock settings, event records and any remote actions while disconnected.
- Define reconnection checks, including which records or settings must be reviewed after service returns.
- Confirm local operating and emergency procedures with the supplier and responsible safety team; never infer them from an app screen.

## Record and Approval

Use a matrix with condition, credential, expected result, observed result, firmware version and reviewer. These are acceptance questions, not claims that a WONLY model supports every feature listed. Keep test credentials and personal access logs out of public reports. Where behaviour differs from the agreed requirement, hold rollout and resolve it with the supplier rather than masking the issue with a permanent online connection.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
