Hotel doors carry more simultaneous requirements than almost any other opening: life-safety code, guest security, acoustic comfort, heavy daily traffic and a design language that matches the property. Getting one specification to satisfy all five is what separates a smooth handover from a costly retrofit.

## Start With Fire Code — It Is Non-Negotiable

Corridor and guest-room doors in hotels almost always require a fire rating — commonly EI 60 for room entrances and EI 90 for higher-risk compartments, confirmed by the project's fire strategy. Specify the certified doorset (leaf, frame, seals and hardware tested together), not just a rated leaf. WONLY's Engineering Fire Doors carry EN 1634 90-minute integrity and comply with Gulf, Southeast Asia and Central Asia codes.

## Layer Security on Top

- A verified burglar-resistance grade for street-level and back-of-house entries
- Multi-point locking and anti-drill cylinders on perimeter doors
- Smart access — RFID or app-based — that integrates with the property system
- Audit-ready access logs for staff and service entrances

## Acoustics Decide Guest Reviews

Corridor noise is a top complaint in hospitality. Specify an acoustic rating for guest-room doors — STC 35+ is a practical benchmark — delivered through a sound-insulating core, perimeter sealing and an automatic drop-seal at the threshold. WONLY doors achieve STC 35+ on applicable series with sealed, barrier-free thresholds.

## Durability at Hotel Traffic Levels

A hotel door cycles far more than a residential one. Demand cycle-test evidence — WONLY tests to 200,000+ open/close cycles — plus a hardware warranty and a spare-parts commitment, because a discontinued handle three years in becomes a corridor-wide aesthetic problem.

## FAQ

Can one door be fire-rated and security-rated? Yes — a properly engineered doorset can carry EN 1634 fire performance and a burglar-resistance grade simultaneously; hotels should specify both for perimeter and service entries.

What should the supplier provide at tender stage? Test certificates for fire and security, acoustic data, cycle-test reports and the compliance pack for the destination market.
