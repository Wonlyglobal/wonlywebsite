Projects are rarely lost on security specs — those are table stakes. They are won on the door that matches the architect's finish schedule, fits a non-standard opening and can carry the developer's own brand. That is a customisation conversation, and cast aluminium is the material best equipped for it.

## Why Casting Unlocks Design

Sheet steel is pressed; cast aluminium is moulded. Casting reproduces deep relief, sculptural panels and fine ornamental detail that stamping cannot, and the surface accepts premium finishing — from powder-coat colours to multi-layer metallic and hand-finished artisan effects — without the corrosion anxiety that limits steel.

## The Customisation Menu

- Finishes: matte black, bronze, champagne, custom RAL colours and realistic wood-grain textures
- Formats: single, double-leaf, pivot and oversized villa entrances in non-standard dimensions
- Glass: clear, frosted or decorative inserts with security glazing
- Hardware: handle styles, smart-lock configurations and display options per series
- Branding: OEM/ODM private label for distributors and developers

## Customisation Without Losing the Certificate

The discipline is keeping bespoke aesthetics inside the tested construction — a certificate applies to the doorset as tested. WONLY engineers custom finishes, sizes and glazing on top of its certified platform (GB 17565 Class A, EN 1634 90-minute fire on applicable series), backed by 1,000+ patents and dedicated artisan capacity in the YIZHAI YISHU series, so a bespoke villa entrance still ships with its ratings intact.

## For Distributors: Customisation as a Margin Strategy

Stock doors compete on price; customised doors compete on fit. Distributors who present finish samples, size flexibility and private-label options move the conversation from cost-per-door to value-per-project — and win hospitality and villa work that catalogue sellers cannot touch.

## FAQ

Does customisation void the security rating? Not when done by the manufacturer within the tested system — always confirm the certificate covers the configuration you order.
