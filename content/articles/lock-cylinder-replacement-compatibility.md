Matching cylinder length does not establish that a replacement will operate the existing lock. Treat the replacement as an interface review, with the actual lock and fittings identified before the purchase is approved.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Record the existing lock-body reference and the intended cylinder profile; do not rely on an end-on photograph alone.
- Ask the supplier to confirm the drive or cam arrangement, fixing and permitted operating configuration.
- Check the proposed inner and outer fittings, protective escutcheon and available space, including any change from the original assembly.
- Identify key/key, thumbturn or other operation and any authorised smart-retrofit interface.
- Separate dimensional compatibility from performance evidence and the requirements of the complete doorset.

## Record and Approval

Build a comparison sheet with existing part, proposed part, each interface, confirmation source, unresolved question and responsible reviewer. A trial must use an authorised installer and agreed acceptance procedure; do not force a component that appears to fit. Reject an unsupported 'universal replacement' description until the relevant interfaces are confirmed.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
