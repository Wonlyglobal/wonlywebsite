An EN 1627 reference should start a document review, not end it. A classification label without a traceable product configuration does not answer whether the proposed supply matches the evidence.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Request document issuer, holder, number, issue date and a legitimate route to verify authenticity.
- Identify the tested or assessed doorset, drawing revision, dimensions and leaf/frame construction.
- Compare locks, hinges, protective fittings, glazing and other relevant components against the proposal.
- Record the supporting wall and fixing details stated in the evidence, and ask how any differences are addressed.
- Separate report observations, classification, certification and project acceptance; ask the issuing body or responsible specialist about uncertain scope.

## Record and Approval

Prepare a two-column matrix: documented configuration versus proposed supply. Add each difference, its supporting assessment, reviewer and release decision. Do not assume a higher-looking label permits arbitrary hardware or size changes. This is a buyer's document-control checklist, not a reproduction of the standard or confirmation that a particular WONLY model is certified.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
