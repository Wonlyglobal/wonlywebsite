A report and a delivered door need a traceable connection. The buyer should be able to follow the proposed configuration through drawings, bill of materials and production records without treating a catalogue name as sufficient identification.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Identify the document's specimen or assessed product and its drawing revision.
- Map that reference to the offered model, controlled bill of materials and hardware set.
- Record manufacturing batch identifiers and how labels link individual doors to the delivery schedule.
- List differences between specimen, approved sample and production supply, with the supporting review for each.
- Agree which records will accompany shipment and which will be retained for later questions.

## Record and Approval

Prepare a traceability chain: evidence reference → approved configuration → production revision → batch → door mark. Include document owner and retrieval location, not confidential manufacturing recipes in a public enquiry. Missing links should trigger clarification before release. A photograph of a label supports identification, but does not replace evidence that the actual configuration is covered.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
