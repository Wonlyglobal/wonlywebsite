An approved sample is useful only when the production order identifies what was approved. Compare a controlled reference against the proposed batch rather than relying on memory or showroom appearance.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Identify the sample, approval revision, photographs and the aspects accepted: appearance, dimensions, function or specific documents.
- Link the sample to the production bill of materials, hardware set, finish reference and drawings.
- Agree inspection characteristics, methods and project-specific acceptance criteria before manufacture.
- Record every proposed difference, why it is needed and who can approve it.
- Define how production findings, corrective actions and reinspection will be closed before shipment release.

## Record and Approval

Use a comparison record with characteristic, approved reference, production observation, evidence, reviewer and disposition. Do not treat a finish sample as approval of security or fire performance. Keep a protected reference sample where practical and control any later changes to it. If multiple suppliers provide interfaces, confirm the combined assembly rather than approving each isolated component and assuming the final door will match.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
