A door variation register makes proposed changes visible before they reach production. It should distinguish a request from an approved instruction and connect both to the affected door marks.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Assign a change identifier and record the approved baseline drawing revision.
- Describe the proposed difference and list affected openings, hardware and interfaces.
- Ask the supplier to identify effects on evidence, cost, delivery and installation.
- Name the reviewer and record approval, rejection or the information still required.
- Release revised documents deliberately and retain the superseded baseline for traceability.

## Record and Approval

A register entry is not permission to manufacture. Define who can approve changes and how that approval reaches purchasing, production and site teams. Close the entry only after the resulting drawings and supplied configuration have been reconciled; avoid overwriting the original decision history.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
