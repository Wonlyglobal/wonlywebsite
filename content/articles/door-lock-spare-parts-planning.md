A spare-parts plan should start with the installed configuration rather than a generic percentage of the order. Hardware revisions and electronic compatibility can matter more than an apparently matching product name.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Record door marks, exact hardware models and relevant firmware or module revisions.
- Ask which seals, hinges and lock modules are serviceable and which require an approved technician.
- Confirm replacement compatibility and whether a change affects the supported assembly.
- Agree storage conditions, shelf life where applicable and supplier handling instructions.
- Record replenishment lead times, reorder references and the responsible service contact.

## Record and Approval

Keep a controlled parts list without including access credentials or key-cutting codes. Ask the supplier to recommend quantities against actual usage, service capability and delivery lead times rather than assuming a universal spare ratio. Review the list after approved product changes and service incidents.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
