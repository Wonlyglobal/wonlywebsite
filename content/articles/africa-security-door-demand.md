Across many African markets, a fast-growing middle class and steady construction are lifting demand for security doors that balance protection, durability and price. For exporters, the region rewards the right value proposition.

## Construction and Urban Growth

New residential and commercial development in cities across the continent creates consistent demand for entrance doors, from value steel doors to premium cast-aluminium for higher-end projects.

## Value and Durability Come First

Buyers prioritise doors that deliver dependable security and long service life for the price. A supplier who can offer strong steel value lines alongside premium options wins a wider share of projects.

## The Rising Role of Smart Locks

As smartphone use spreads, interest in fingerprint and app locks is growing in urban and premium segments, adding an upsell path on top of the core door business.
