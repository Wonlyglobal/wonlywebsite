WONLY Security Team has achieved a **GUINNESS WORLD RECORDS™ title for the longest lockpicking livestream (video)**. The record attempt, organized by Wangli Security & Surveillance Product Co., Ltd., concluded in Yongkang, Zhejiang, China, on 10 September 2026.

The recognition followed a fully public technical challenge staged from 9 to 10 September. According to the event record supplied by WONLY, 24 certified locksmiths took turns examining and attempting to open the selected WONLY security-lock configuration during a continuous livestream lasting more than 24 hours. No participant achieved a successful technical opening within the challenge period and rules.

## A Public Test, Not a Closed-Door Demonstration

The purpose of the event was to make physical-security performance observable. Rather than presenting a laboratory result without context, WONLY placed the lock system in front of professional challengers and a live audience for an extended, uninterrupted test.

Lock resistance is only one part of entrance security. Cylinder design, protective hardware, the lock case, door leaf, frame, installation and the surrounding opening all contribute to real-world performance. The challenge therefore should not be read as a claim that any lock is absolutely impossible to defeat. It records what happened with the tested configuration, participants, methods, rules and duration.

For project buyers, that distinction matters. Security claims are most useful when they are attached to a defined product configuration and supported by repeatable evidence. WONLY applies the same principle across its [security-door portfolio](/products/security-doors/), combining physical protection with engineering review, manufacturing control and project documentation.

## A Challenge Tradition Dating Back to 2003

WONLY's public lock-challenge programme began in 2003 with the “Challenge the Lock-Picking King” campaign. Over the years, the reward attached to successive challenges grew from RMB 10,000 to RMB 1 million, while new product generations were placed under professional scrutiny.

The 2026 livestream extended that tradition into a transparent digital format. A continuous public broadcast created a visible timeline and allowed the challenge to be followed beyond the venue. The resulting GUINNESS WORLD RECORDS™ title recognizes the duration of the livestream itself; the unsuccessful opening attempts are a documented outcome of the event.

## What the Result Means for WONLY

For WONLY, the achievement is a milestone in a longer commitment to tested security. It reflects three priorities:

- **Openness:** product performance should be demonstrated in conditions that customers and professionals can observe.
- **Engineering discipline:** claims should relate to a specific construction, lock system and test boundary.
- **Continuous improvement:** every challenge should feed back into product design, production control and future validation.

These priorities also shape WONLY's work in [smart locks](/products/smart-locks/) and integrated entrance systems. Electronic access features can improve convenience and management, but they must be supported by dependable mechanical protection, controlled failure behaviour and a well-specified door assembly.

## Raising the Standard for Residential Entrance Security

The record does not end the testing process. WONLY will continue to use professional evaluation, product standards and real-world challenge formats to improve security-door and lock performance. For distributors, developers and project teams, the practical message is simple: compare complete configurations, ask for evidence that matches the supplied model, and define acceptance criteria before procurement.
