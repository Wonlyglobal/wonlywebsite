Handing abbreviations can be interpreted differently across markets and product families. A written label is useful only when both parties agree on the viewing side and the actual movement.

## Practical Review Checklist

- Mark the viewing position on the plan and distinguish inside from outside.
- Show hinge side, leaf movement and the full swing arc; identify active and inactive leaves on pairs.
- Locate nearby walls, furniture and access devices that could obstruct the intended movement.
- Ask the supplier to return the same sketch with its model-specific handing designation.
- Require the designer and buyer to approve the returned drawing before the order is frozen.

## Record and Approval

Create a register containing door mark, viewing side, hinge side, arrow sketch, leaf sequence, supplier designation and approval revision. Do not resolve a conflicting abbreviation by choosing a familiar regional convention. Where escape, accessibility or fire requirements matter, the responsible project team must confirm the permitted direction and hardware; this article does not set a local code rule.

Send the door marks, quantities, destination and the completed review record to WONLY for a configuration-specific discussion. Confirm availability, evidence and responsibilities in writing; this checklist is not a product certificate or a universal installation instruction.

## FAQ

**Can this record replace the approved specification?** No. It organises the questions; the responsible designer, supplier and installer must resolve the actual configuration before release.
